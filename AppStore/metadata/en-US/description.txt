Jelly brings your Jellyfin library to Apple TV – native, fast and in original quality. All you need is your own Jellyfin server (free at jellyfin.org).

ORIGINAL, NOT CONVERTED
Jelly plays your files as they are: MKV, MP4, AVI and TS with H.264, HEVC, AV1, VC-1 or MPEG-2. Your server doesn't have to re-encode anything, and the picture stays untouched.

DOLBY VISION, HDR10+ AND ATMOS
Dolby Vision (profiles 5 and 8) and HDR10+ play through Apple's player with dynamic metadata, Dolby Atmos in Dolby Digital Plus reaches your receiver as a bitstream, and TrueHD and DTS-HD play losslessly as multichannel audio. HDR10 and HLG work for everything else. Frame rate and dynamic range adapt to every film.

SUBTITLES AS THEY WERE MEANT TO BE
ASS/SSA subtitles with embedded fonts, positions and effects, plus PGS, VobSub, SRT and external subtitles. You choose size, colour and background.

SIGN IN WITHOUT TYPING
Jelly finds your server on your home network by itself. Profiles without a password open with one click, all others with a code from the Jellyfin app on your phone. Several servers and profiles on one Apple TV, with "Who's watching?" at launch.

MADE FOR TV SHOWS
Continue watching and next episodes on the home screen and full-screen in the Top Shelf. Jelly detects intros and credits by their sound – even without a server plugin – and starts the next episode as soon as the credits roll.

ON THE GO AND ON SLOW CONNECTIONS
Jelly measures the connection to your server and picks the best quality that plays without stuttering. If the connection drops mid-film, Jelly adapts and goes back to the better quality later. At home, Jelly talks to your server directly.

DISCOVER WITH JELLYSEERR
Connect your Jellyseerr and request films and shows that aren't on your server yet – with trends, new releases, search and live download progress.

Also: ratings on detail pages, chapters, thumbnail previews while scrubbing, audio and subtitle sync, a connection analysis and a cover art screensaver like iTunes used to have.

TRY IT FREE FOR 2 WEEKS
Try Jelly with all features free for 2 weeks. After that, the Jelly annual subscription renews every year at the price shown in the App Store for your country. One subscription covers Jelly on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad with your Apple ID. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically for another year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends; you can cancel at any time in the Apple TV settings under Users and Accounts › Subscriptions.
