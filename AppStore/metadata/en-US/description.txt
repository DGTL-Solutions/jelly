Jelly is the Jellyfin player for everyone who wants to see their films the way they are: in original quality, without your server having to transcode – with Dolby Vision, Atmos and fully styled subtitles. All you need is your own Jellyfin server (free at jellyfin.org).

WHAT MAKES JELLY DIFFERENT
• The original, not a transcode: MKV, MP4, AVI and TS with H.264, HEVC, AV1, VC-1 or MPEG-2. Whatever the device can't decode itself, Jelly decodes – your server doesn't have to re-encode anything, so even a small NAS is enough.
• Subtitles as they were meant to be: ASS/SSA with embedded fonts, positions and effects, plus PGS, VobSub, SRT and external subtitles. You choose size, color and background.
• Made for TV shows: Jelly detects intros and credits by their sound – no server plugin needed – and starts the next episode as soon as the credits roll.
• Smart on slow connections: Jelly measures the connection and first drops unused audio tracks – losslessly – before the server scales the picture down. At home, Jelly talks to your server directly.
• Jellyseerr built in: request movies and shows, search, and follow the download live.
• One subscription for everything: Jelly on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID.

ON APPLE TV
Dolby Vision (profiles 5 and 8) and HDR10+ play through Apple's player with dynamic metadata, Dolby Atmos in Dolby Digital Plus reaches your receiver as a bitstream, and TrueHD and DTS-HD play losslessly as multichannel audio. Frame rate and dynamic range adapt to every film. Continue watching and next episodes appear full-screen in the Top Shelf, plus a cover art screensaver like iTunes used to have.

SIGN IN WITHOUT TYPING
Jelly finds your server on your home network by itself. Profiles without a password open with one click, all others with a code from the Jellyfin app on your phone. Several servers and profiles, with "Who's watching?" at launch.

Also: ratings on detail pages, chapters, thumbnail previews while scrubbing, audio and subtitle sync and detailed playback information.
