Jelly 2.0 – the biggest update so far.

• Sign in your Apple TV with the iPhone: scan the QR code, confirm, done.
• Watch together: SharePlay over FaceTime and SyncPlay with everyone on your server.
• From iPhone to Apple TV: pick on your iPhone, play on the Apple TV and control it from there.
• Kids profile with an age rating and a PIN.
• Combine filters: genre, decade, rating.
• New: a Continue Watching widget, Siri and Shortcuts (“Continue watching in Jelly”), Handoff between your devices.
• Jelly is now on the Mac too.
• The next episode starts without waiting.
• “Your request has arrived”: a notice when a Jellyseerr request is available.
• Optional: theme music, automatic downloads of the next episodes, and notifications when a request arrives.
