Jelly is the Jellyfin player for everyone who wants to see their films the way they are: in original quality, without your server having to transcode – on iPhone and iPad, with Picture in Picture and AirPlay. All you need is your own Jellyfin server (free at jellyfin.org).

WHAT MAKES JELLY DIFFERENT
• The original, not a transcode: MKV, MP4, AVI and TS with H.264, HEVC, AV1, VC-1 or MPEG-2. Whatever the device can't decode itself, Jelly decodes – your server doesn't have to re-encode anything, so even a small NAS is enough.
• Subtitles as they were meant to be: ASS/SSA with embedded fonts, positions and effects, plus PGS, VobSub, SRT and external subtitles. You choose size, colour and background.
• Made for TV shows: Jelly detects intros and credits by their sound – no server plugin needed – and starts the next episode as soon as the credits roll.
• Smart on slow connections: Jelly measures the connection and first drops unused audio tracks – losslessly – before the server scales the picture down. At home, Jelly talks to your server directly.
• Jellyseerr built in: request movies and shows, search, and follow the download live.
• One subscription for everything: Jelly on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID.

ON IPHONE AND IPAD
Browse your library with large artwork, double-tap to skip 10 seconds, scrub with thumbnail previews and pinch to fill the screen. Picture in Picture keeps the movie playing while you use other apps, AirPlay sends it to your TV, and the Lock Screen shows what's playing. On iPad, Jelly uses the whole screen with a sidebar and wider layouts.

EASY SIGN-IN
Jelly finds your server on your home network. Sign in with your password or with Quick Connect – several servers and profiles, with "Who's watching?" at launch.

Also: ratings on detail pages, chapters, thumbnail previews while scrubbing, audio and subtitle sync and detailed playback information.
