Jelly is a client for Jellyfin, the free and open-source media server that people run on their own computer or NAS. The app contains no content of its own; it plays the user's own library from the user's own server.

HOW TO REVIEW (public demo server of the Jellyfin project, public-domain and Creative Commons films only)
1. Launch Jelly. In the field "Server Address", enter: demo.jellyfin.org/stable and tap "Connect".
2. Choose the profile "demo", leave the password empty and tap "Sign In".
3. Browse the library and play e.g. "Caminandes: Llama Drama". Tap the video for the controls; the options button opens subtitles, audio, quality and info. Picture in Picture and AirPlay are in the top bar.

SUBSCRIPTION (Guideline 3.1.2)
- The app is free to download and unlocked by the auto-renewable subscription "Jelly Annual" (1 year, 9.99 EUR) with a 2-week free trial as introductory offer. The same subscription unlocks Jelly on Apple TV (universal purchase).
- The offer appears once after signing in and whenever playback is started without an active subscription. It states the price per year, the trial, automatic renewal and how to cancel, and links to the Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and the Privacy Policy.
- Settings (profile button at the top right on iPhone, Settings tab on iPad) > Annual Subscription shows the status and offers subscribing, "Restore Purchases" and managing the subscription. Browsing the library works without a subscription.

NOTES
- Local network access is used only to discover Jellyfin servers in the user's home network (TCP 8096) and to reach the server directly at home.
- App Transport Security exception: home media servers are usually reached via plain HTTP on the local network (e.g. http://192.168.1.10:8096) or user-chosen host names, so per-domain exceptions are not possible.
- Background audio mode: used only so that playback continues in Picture in Picture and on AirPlay.
- The "Discover" tab is optional. It connects to the user's own Jellyseerr server, a companion request manager for Jellyfin; without one it only explains how to connect it. The demo server has no Jellyseerr.
- No data is collected; there is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The interface is in English and German and follows the device language.
