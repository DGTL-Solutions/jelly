# Jelly – Help and Contact

Jelly plays your own Jellyfin library on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad – in original quality, with Dolby Vision and Atmos. · [Deutsch](../support/)

## Frequently asked questions

- **What do I need?** Your own Jellyfin server (free at [jellyfin.org](https://jellyfin.org)) and an Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later, or an iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later.
- **What does Jelly cost?** The first 2 weeks are free. After that, the Jelly annual subscription costs the price shown in the App Store for your country, per year. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
- **How do I cancel?** On Apple TV: Settings › Users and Accounts › your name › Subscriptions › Jelly. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
- **New device?** In Jelly, go to Settings › Annual subscription › "Restore Purchases". One subscription covers Jelly on every Apple TV, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID.
- **Refunds?** Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **A film stutters or won't start?** Settings › Connection shows what the connection to your server can handle. You can report problems at [github.com/DGTL-Solutions/jelly/issues](https://github.com/DGTL-Solutions/jelly/issues).
