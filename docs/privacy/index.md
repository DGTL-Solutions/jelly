# Privacy Policy for Jelly (Apple TV, iPhone and iPad)

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · [Deutsche Fassung](../datenschutz/)

Seller on the App Store: Nessy GmbH, Kirchstraße 10, 53773 Hennef, Germany, phone +49 2242 9329301, email: info@dgtl24.net
Rights holder: DGTL Solutions GmbH

## In short

Jelly does not collect personal data. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no account with us. Neither Nessy GmbH nor DGTL Solutions GmbH receives any data about you through the app.

## Who Jelly talks to

- **Your Jellyfin server:** Jelly only connects to the servers you set up yourself. Your sign-in, what you play, your playback progress and your audio and subtitle choices go there, so that "Continue Watching" is right on all your devices. The operator of the server is responsible for this data.
- **Your Jellyseerr server (optional):** If you connect it, your sign-in, searches and requests go there. When you sign in to Jellyfin with a user name and password, Jelly signs the same account in to a Jellyseerr that demonstrably belongs to that Jellyfin server. The password is used once for this and is not stored.
- **Apple (subscription):** You take out the Jelly annual subscription and its trial with your Apple ID. Apple handles payment, renewal and cancellation under its own privacy policy; Jelly only checks the subscription status on the device and does not pass it on.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB):** Jelly loads images in the Discover tab from image.tmdb.org. TMDB sees your IP address.
- **Local network:** To find your server without typing, Jelly looks for Jellyfin servers on your home network.

## What is stored on your device

- Session keys for your servers (in the device keychain) – no passwords.
- Your settings, the most recently loaded home screen rows, the results of intro and credits detection and measurements of your connection speed.
- A diagnostics log for troubleshooting, without passwords or access keys. It never leaves the device and can be deleted under Settings › Diagnostics.

When you delete Jelly, the system removes this data.

## Your rights

As we do not process any data about you, there is nothing we could disclose. Please direct questions about data on your Jellyfin or Jellyseerr server to its operator. Questions about this policy: info@dgtl24.net.
